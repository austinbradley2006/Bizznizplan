## Problem & Opportunity

### Customer pain points
- Clutter and bulk waste pile up; municipal pickup limits what can go curbside.
- National franchises quote high minimums and opaque pricing.
- People want same-week pickup, honest estimates, and proof items were disposed of responsibly.

### Why now
- [ ] Housing turnover / move season in your market
- [ ] Aging housing stock → estate cleanouts and downsizing
- [ ] Local competitors with weak reviews on [price / scheduling / professionalism]
- [ ] Side-hustle → full-time path if volume supports a truck + helper

### Robinhood brand angle
Use the name as a **fair-dealing promise**, not a costume:
- **Transparent quotes** before load — price tied to volume, not mystery fees.
- **Community give-back** — donate usable furniture/appliances when possible; share receipts or partner names.
- **Accessible pricing** — senior/military/first-responder discount policy (define % in Financials).

> ⚠️ **Trademark note:** "Robinhood" is heavily associated with Robinhood Markets (finance). Before final branding, run a state-level business name search and consider variants (*Robin Hood Junk*, *Hood Junk Removal*, *Sherwood Hauling*) if counsel flags conflict.
