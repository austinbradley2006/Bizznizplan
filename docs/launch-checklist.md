# Launch Checklist

## Before first paid job
- [ ] LLC / business entity filed
- [ ] Business bank account
- [ ] Liability + auto insurance active
- [ ] Google Business Profile live with photos
- [ ] Price sheet finalized
- [ ] Service agreement / waiver template (damage, access, payment terms)
- [ ] Dump facility account or fee schedule understood
- [ ] Branded invoice or receipt method

## First 30 days
- [ ] Complete 10 jobs (even at discount) for reviews
- [ ] Log actual dump fees vs estimates
- [ ] Track drive time + load time per job type
- [ ] Ask every customer: "How did you find us?"
- [ ] Adjust pricing if margin < target

## First 90 days
- [ ] 25+ Google reviews (goal)
- [ ] 2 B2B referral partners (realtor, property manager, or contractor)
- [ ] Break-even on monthly fixed costs
- [ ] Decide: add helper vs add marketing spend
